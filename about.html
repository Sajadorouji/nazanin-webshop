The story behind Naz Petit Bakery 🥐

Naz Petit Bakery started when I was looking for a new hobby to brighten my days during some difficult moments in life. I thought baking might be a nice little distraction. I didn't expect to fall in love with it quite so much… especially considering the absolutely ridiculous number of dishes it creates!

Soon, I started sharing my bakes with friends. Their enthusiasm and kind words gave me the confidence to keep going, try new recipes, and dream a little bigger.

Then came my first order: a birthday cake for a friend. She insisted on being my very first customer so that, one day, when Naz Petit Bakery became famous, she could proudly say she was there from the beginning. 😂

That moment meant more to me than she probably realized. It made me dream of creating pastries that do more than taste good: pastries that become part of someone's special celebrations, or bring a little sweetness to a difficult day.

And that's how Naz Petit Bakery began — with a little curiosity, a lot of love for baking, far too many dishes, and the dream of making people's days a little sweeter. 🤎
