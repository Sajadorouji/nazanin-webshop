Order & Delivery

How to get your sweet bites.

A little something made just for you ♡

Every order at Naz Petit Bakery is made fresh, with care and a little extra love. Whether you're planning a celebration, bringing something to a gathering, or simply treating yourself, we'd love to bake something for you!

Here's everything you need to know about placing an order.

1. How to order

Ordering is easy! Just send us a message through Instagram or Telegram.

When getting in touch, please include:

  • What you'd like: Products and quantities.
  • When you need it: Your preferred date and approximate time.
  • How you'd like to receive it: Collection or delivery.
  • Your address: If you'd like delivery.
  • Any special requests: Let us know about allergies or other requirements.

We'll get back to you to discuss availability, confirm the details and share the total price.

Your order is only confirmed once we've agreed on all the details.

2. When should I order?

Everything is made fresh to order, so a little planning helps us give your treats the attention they deserve.

Here are our minimum order notice periods:

Product Order in advance
Cakes At least 7 days
Cookies At least 1 day
Cream puffs At least 4 days
Éclairs At least 4 days
Macarons At least 4 days

Have something last-minute in mind? Feel free to ask! We may be able to accommodate your request depending on our availability.

For larger orders or special requests, please get in touch as early as possible.

3. Collection & Delivery

We offer both collection and delivery options.

Collection

You can collect your order from our designated pickup location during an agreed time window, or arrange collection directly from our home.

Home collection details and the address will be shared privately once your order is confirmed.

Please arrange your collection time with us in advance.

Delivery

We deliver within Amsterdam and surrounding areas.

  • Delivery fee: €5
  • Free delivery on orders above €100.

Your delivery address and preferred time will be discussed when placing your order. Delivery availability depends on the location and schedule.

4. Payment

We want to keep things simple!

We accept:

  • Bank transfer / Tikkie
  • Cash (for collection orders)

For collection orders: Payment is made upon collection.

For delivery orders:

  • Orders up to €50: Payment upon delivery.
  • Orders above €50: A 50% deposit is required in advance to confirm your order. The remaining balance is paid upon delivery.

5. Changes & Cancellations

We understand that plans can change!

  • Last-minute orders: Accepted only if availability allows.
  • Order changes: Please let us know as early as possible. Changes can be accommodated before preparation begins.
  • Cancellations: You can cancel your order before preparation starts. Once preparation has begun, cancellations and refunds are no longer possible.

If you need to make any changes, just send us a message.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I place an order for the same day?

You can always ask! While our products have different minimum preparation times, we may occasionally have availability for last-minute requests.

Can I order a custom cake?

Absolutely! Get in touch with your ideas, preferred flavours, size and occasion. We'll discuss what's possible and provide a quote.

Can I mix different products in one order?

Of course! You can combine your favourite treats. Just keep in mind that preparation times may vary depending on the products and quantities.

Can I choose a specific delivery time?

You can share your preferred time when ordering. We'll do our best to accommodate it, subject to availability.

Do you offer gluten-free or vegan products?

At the moment, we don't offer dedicated gluten-free or vegan options.

What if I have a food allergy?

Please let us know about any allergies before placing your order. We may be able to accommodate certain requests depending on the product. However, we cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.

Can I order for a special occasion or a large gathering?

Of course! Whether it's a birthday, dinner party, celebration or just an excuse to get together, we'd love to be part of it. Please contact us in advance so we can discuss your requirements.

Where can I find you?

We're based in the Amsterdam area. Our exact pickup details will be shared when your order is confirmed.

Have something delicious in mind?

We'd love to hear from you. Send us a message, tell us what you're craving, and let's make it happen!
